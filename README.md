# PvP Coach — Minecraft Mod

I built a Minecraft mod called **PvP Coach** that helps players improve their PvP skills through practice, useful tips, and real-time statistics.

The mod can spawn a **PvP practice bot**, similar to a zombie, that players can fight to improve their aim, movement, timing, and combat skills. While practicing, PvP Coach provides useful information such as **Keystrokes, FPS, CPS, and target distance**. The target-distance indicator helps players understand how far away their opponent is and whether they are within a range where they can successfully hit them.

The goal of **PvP Coach** is to make PvP practice easier and more interactive by giving players feedback while they fight instead of simply practicing without knowing what they need to improve.

### Main Features

* 🥊 PvP practice bot
* ⌨️ Keystrokes display
* 🎯 Target distance indicator
* 🖱️ CPS counter
* 📊 FPS counter
* 💡 PvP tips and improvement advice
* 🏃 Practice movement, aim, timing, and combat skills

**PvP Coach** is designed to be a lightweight PvP-training mod that helps players understand their mistakes and gradually become better at Minecraft PvP.
